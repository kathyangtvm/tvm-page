Daniel Tsai蔡明忠

Chairman, Fubon Group and Taiwan Mobile

Eldest son of the late Tsai Wan-tsai, founder of the Fubon Group. He joined Fubon in 1981 and has chaired Fubon Insurance, Fubon Financial Holding and Taipei Fubon Bank; in 2003 he led the acquisition of a Hong Kong bank, now Fubon Bank (Hong Kong).

Today he chairs the Fubon Group, Taiwan Mobile and Fubon Media, which runs the e-commerce platform momo. A Georgetown Law graduate (LL.M., 1979), he gave US$30 million for the new Daniel Tsai Hall, the largest gift in the law school’s history, and received an honorary doctorate there in 2025.

Not to be confused with his younger brother Richard Tsai, who chairs Fubon Financial Holding.

富邦集團董事長、台灣大哥大董事長

富邦集團已故總裁蔡萬才長子，1981 年加入富邦，曾任富邦產險、富邦金控、台北富邦銀行董事長；2003 年主導收購香港港基銀行（今富邦銀行（香港））。

現任富邦集團、台灣大哥大、富邦媒體科技（momo）董事長。喬治城大學法學碩士，捐贈 3,000 萬美元興建 Daniel Tsai Hall，創該法學院最高捐款紀錄，2025 年獲頒名譽法學博士；2026 年進入東海大學董事會。

英文名為 Daniel Tsai。

Chang Kuo-chun張果軍

Founder and Chairman, Storm Media Group

Formerly head of Goldman Sachs’ Taipei branch, chairman of Fubon Securities and an adviser to Fubon Financial. He studied sociology at National Taiwan University and holds an MBA from Columbia.

He founded the online news outlet Storm Media in 2014 and in 2017 bought The Journalist, a political weekly founded in 1987, which moved fully online in 2021.

風傳媒創辦人暨董事長

曾任高盛台北分公司總經理、富邦證券董事長、富邦金控顧問；台大社會系畢業、美國哥倫比亞大學 MBA。

2014 年創辦網路媒體《風傳媒》，2017 年買下 1987 年創刊的《新新聞》周刊，該刊 2021 年停印紙本、轉為線上經營。

Wen Lee李效文

Chairman and President, HiTRUST

Chairman and President of HiTRUST, a Taipei company founded in March 1998.

HiTRUST provides digital banking, cybersecurity, online payment and AI-based fraud prevention services to banks and retailers, and was an early partner of Visa and Mastercard on e-commerce payments in Asia.

網際威信（HiTRUST）董事長兼總經理

網際威信1998 年 3 月創立，專做數位金融、資安、電商金流與 AI 防詐，早年即與 VISA、MasterCard 合作開發亞洲電商金流服務。

Cheng Chun-chieh成群傑

Chairman, Da Tsinghua Investment

A 1979 industrial chemistry graduate of National Tsing Hua University, he spent seven years at Formosa Plastics before moving into capital markets in 1988, becoming one of Taiwan’s early angel investors. The university named him a distinguished alumnus in 2014.

In 2011 he brought together Tsing Hua alumni entrepreneurs to launch the Da Tsinghua Fund, and he heads several investment firms. He also backs Taiwanese television, and is credited as a presenter of the medical drama Wake Up 2 (2017).

大清華投資、新銳資本董事長

1979 年清大工業化學系畢業，在台塑工作七年後，1988 年轉入資本市場，是台灣早期的天使投資人；2014 年獲清大傑出校友。

2011 年號召清華校友企業家成立「大清華基金」，旗下有多家投資公司。也投入影視，為公視《麻醉風暴2》出品人之一。

Yang Pan-chyr楊泮池

Former President, National Taiwan University

A chest physician and former President of National Taiwan University, he is an academician of Academia Sinica, Taiwan’s national academy. Early in his career he spent two years practising medicine in Saudi Arabia.

In March 2026, after two terms as vice president, he became President of the Institute for Biotechnology and Medicine Industry, where he champions new drug development and AI in healthcare. He also joined the board of Tunghai University in 2026, alongside Daniel Tsai.

前台大校長、中研院院士

前台大校長、胸腔醫學專家，現任中研院院士；早年曾派駐沙烏地阿拉伯行醫兩年。

曾任兩屆生策會副會長，2026 年 3 月接任會長，主推「新藥＋AI 醫療」；同年也與蔡明忠一起進入東海大學董事會。

Lin Shih-hua林石化

Nephrologist; former Superintendent, Tri-Service General Hospital

Raised in a farming family in Pingtung, he served as Superintendent of Tri-Service General Hospital and President of the National Defense Medical Center, holding the rank of major general. In 2018 he became the first general to receive Taiwan’s national teaching award.

A specialist in kidney disease and electrolytes, he is known for work on AI-assisted ECG reading that can flag abnormal blood potassium within seconds.

腎臟科權威、前三總院長

屏東農家出身，曾任三軍總醫院院長、國防醫學院院長，官拜少將；2018 年獲教育部師鐸獎，是軍醫史上首位獲獎的將軍。

腎臟與電解質專家，投入 AI 輔助心電圖研究，可在數秒內判讀血鉀異常。

Weng Ming-cheng翁明正

Co-founder, Millerful Capital

Born in Tainan and educated at National Taiwan University and the University of Southern California, he began his career as a management trainee at Citibank.

He went on to run Salomon Smith Barney’s Taipei branch, chaired Lehman Brothers Securities in Taiwan, where he advised on several landmark bank mergers, and from 2010 headed Nomura Securities in Taiwan. He later co-founded Millerful Capital, a real estate investment firm behind a Taiwan-listed REIT, together with Chang Kuo-chun.

樂富資本共同創辦人

台南人，台南一中、台大、美國南加大畢業，從花旗銀行儲備幹部做起。

曾任花旗所羅門美邦證券台北分公司總經理、雷曼兄弟證券董事長暨台灣區負責人（經手多起國內金融併購案），2010 年接任野村證券台灣區總裁；後與張果軍等人共同創辦樂富資本，推動不動產投資信託（REIT）。

Lou Pei-jen婁培人

Head and neck cancer surgeon, National Taiwan University Hospital

An ear, nose and throat surgeon specialising in head and neck cancer, and one of the pioneers of photodynamic therapy in Taiwan. He served as Vice Superintendent of National Taiwan University Hospital until his term ended in July 2026.

He has served as President of the Taiwan Head and Neck Society and received the Ministry of Education’s national teaching award.

台大醫院耳鼻喉科權威、前副院長

耳鼻喉科權威，專長頭頸部腫瘤與癌症治療，是台灣光動力治療的先驅之一；曾任台大醫院副院長，2026 年 7 月任期屆滿卸任。

曾任台灣頭頸部腫瘤醫學會理事長，並獲教育部師鐸獎。

Bill Yeh葉清澤

Chairman, Lan Fa Textile

Chairman of Lan Fa Textile, a polyester textured yarn maker founded in 1972. It now produces functional and recycled yarns at its plant in Hangzhou, and in 2025 leased its former Zhubei factory to Wistron for AI server production.

He has close ties with the Wang family of Formosa Plastics: he was a long-standing friend of the late founder Wang Yung-ching, and serves as an independent director of Formosa Plastics.

聯發紡織董事長

聯發紡織 1972 年成立，專做聚酯加工絲；目前生產集中在杭州廠，以機能性與環保紗為主，2025 年起將竹北廠整廠出租給緯創作為 AI 伺服器基地。

與台塑王家交情深厚，曾是王永慶的忘年之交，也擔任台塑獨立董事。

Kevin Yung

Chief Financial Officer, Rosewood Hotel Group

Group CFO since June 2023, overseeing finance across Rosewood Hotel Group’s brands, including Rosewood, New World Hotels & Resorts, Asaya and Carlyle & Co. He is also Managing Partner of Rosewood Capital Ventures.

Previously CEO of Chow Tai Fook Education Group in Hong Kong, he spent nearly a decade in finance and investment in Beijing, and began his career at Morgan Stanley and later Citigroup in New York. He sits on the board of Shih Hsin University in Taipei.

Rosewood Hotel Group 財務長

2023 年 6 月接任集團財務長，負責 Rosewood、New World、Asaya、Carlyle & Co 等品牌財務；同時兼任 Rosewood Capital Ventures 管理合夥人。

曾任香港周大福教育集團執行長，並在北京從事金融與投資近十年；職涯起步於紐約摩根士丹利，後任花旗副總裁。現任台北世新大學董事。
